Pulls the real, current top-of-ladder leaderboard for the selected region and keeps only Eternus VI players (badge 116), the smallest, highest tier the ladder has, one step above the pros. If the API turns out not to subdivide Eternus into subranks, this automatically falls back to all of Eternus and says so in the status line, rather than silently returning nothing.

"Scan Now" checks the live match feed and sorts by the game's own match score, the same value that puts a match in the #1 Watch slot in-game. It shows you the top 5 live right now, and quietly logs the #1 match's ID to a running list in your browser. Over days/weeks that list becomes a real sample of top-lobby games. Hit "Analyze Logged Matches" any time to see who performs best across everything logged so far. Only matches that have actually finished can be analyzed; ones still in progress are skipped until they wrap up.
KDA = (kills + assists) ÷ deaths, pooled across every game rather than averaged per game, so a single lucky match cannot distort it. This is the formula Dota uses. It is preferred over raw K/D here because Deadlock is 6v6 and teamfight heavy, meaning assists carry real signal and pure K/D would undervalue setup players.
Role Score = how a player compares to the average player on their own archetype (Assassin, Brawler, Marksman, Mystic), using Deadlock's own internal hero classification read from the game files. 1.00x is exactly average for that role. Baselines are computed from the games you have actually loaded rather than invented in advance, so they self calibrate as your dataset grows.
Team Share = a player's net worth ÷ the average of their own five teammates, not the whole lobby. This isolates whether someone is the engine of their team from whether their team is simply winning. Hover any Team Share value to see it split by win and loss. A player whose Team Share barely drops in losses is carrying regardless of outcome, while one who only looks strong in wins may be riding the snowball.
Net Worth Share compares a player to all 12 players in the match. It cancels out game length but not team dominance, since net worth inflates for everyone on a winning team. Damage / Min and Dmg Share work the same way for hero damage, which counts damage to heroes only and not creeps or objectives, so it favours fight focused players by design. Read it alongside Team Share rather than alone. KP% = (kills + assists) ÷ your team's total kills that game, a team relative measure of involvement that holds up whether a game had 20 total kills or 80.
Data source: api.deadlock-api.com, a community run, open source, unofficial API.
